Market Terms Explained

Trend

Trend shows the primary direction based on recent price structure.
Primary Uptrend = higher highs & higher lows.

Volatility

Crypto can move quickly.
Use position sizing and stop loss discipline.

Historical Ranges

Historical ranges show how much the asset moved in similar conditions over different time periods.
These are probabilities, not predictions.

Top Movers

Highlights the biggest daily gainers and losers.
Useful to identify momentum or panic.
