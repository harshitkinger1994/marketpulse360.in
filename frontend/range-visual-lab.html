Range Visual Lab

Open. Low. High. Current. One clean visual that tells the story fast.

This is a test page for the new daily range component. The idea is to show where price is sitting inside the day range, how far it has moved from the open, and whether the session tone is green or red at a glance.

Open marker Low-high session rail Current price pin Green/red directional fill Position inside day range

Best use on market pages

Perfect for index cards, stock search results, watchlists, telegram alerts, and exchange pages where quick intraday context matters.

Why this is stronger

Numbers alone force the eye to work too hard. This turns the day into a visual map: range, control, rejection, expansion, compression, and recovery.

What we are testing

Color balance, marker clarity, density, and whether the current pin feels premium enough to use across India, global, commodity, and crypto pages.
